## Ground rules for all campaign material

1. **Fan-made, always disclosed.** Every bio, page footer, and video description says "unofficial fan project — not affiliated with Lucasfilm/Disney." No monetized campaign spend on Star Wars IP; growth is organic + community. (AdSense already on the site is the owner's existing call; campaign adds no paid ads.)
2. **One brand voice:** enthusiastic superfan who builds things — knowledgeable, playful, never snarky at other fans.
3. **Handles are fixed** (see social-media-playbook.md). Never mint new ones; the brand already survived one identity split.
4. **Everything links back to starwars.guide.** The hub is the front door; subdomain apps are rooms.
5. **Growth goal is social followers and subscribers**, not an email list — no newsletter. Every surface (site, apps, videos) pushes follows on Threads, BlueSky, Instagram, and YouTube.
