# Social Media Playbook

*Per-platform strategy for AurebeshFiles. Drafted 2026-07-21.*

**This is the primary growth channel.** There is no newsletter/email list by design — followers and subscribers on the platforms below are the goal, and every piece of content ends with a reason to follow ("new character every Tuesday", "new score challenge every Saturday"). The series formats exist precisely so a follow has a predictable payoff.

Every bio: one-line brand promise + "unofficial fan project" + link to starwars.guide (the hub, never a subdomain — let the hub route them).

## Weekly cadence (sustainable for one person, batchable)

| Day | Post | Platforms |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | SWordle week kickoff / streak prompt ("Who's still alive?") | Threads, BlueSky |
| Tue | Character spotlight (Arc 2 series): carousel + 30s vertical clip | IG, YT Shorts, Threads |
| Wed | HyperPanels "panel of the day" matched to whatever fandom is arguing about | Threads, BlueSky, IG |
| Thu | Builder/dev-log post: screenshot, metric, or "how it works" thread | Threads, BlueSky (crosspost best ones to r/SideProject) |
| Fri | Poll: next week's spotlight character / Clone Defense planet vote | Threads, BlueSky, IG Stories |
| Sat | Clone Defense clip: one dramatic run moment, score on screen | YT Shorts, IG Reels, TikTok if opened |
| Sun | Rest, or reshare the week's best performer | — |

Batch production: one sitting per month for Tuesday clips, one for Saturday clips. Text posts written weekly in 30 minutes.

## Per-platform notes

### Threads + BlueSky (primary)
- Conversational, reply-heavy. Join canon arguments and *settle them with a timeline deep-link* — the product demo disguised as being helpful.
- Post results-grid replies when people share SWordle scores; always reply to anyone who @s the brand.
- BlueSky's SW community skews lore-nerd: lean Timeline/character content there; Threads gets more game/meme content.

### Instagram
- Carousels: "Every [character] appearance in canon order" (Arc 2), "10 comic panels that answer any text message" (HyperPanels).
- Reels: Clone Defense runs, timeline scroll-throughs with music.
- Stories: daily SWordle "I got it in 3" screenshot + poll stickers. Link sticker always to starwars.guide with UTM.

### YouTube
- Shorts: same verticals as Reels — zero extra cost.
- Long-form (monthly, optional): "I built a Star Wars tower defense with zero dependencies" dev retrospective; "The complete Star Wars timeline explained in 12 minutes" using the app as the visual. Long-form is the discoverability moonshot; Shorts are the floor.

### Reddit (participation, not promotion)
- Rule: 10 genuine comments per 1 link. Only link when it directly answers the thread ("here's every event of 19 BBY on one page").
- Launch posts (Clone Defense, keyboard) go to r/WebGames, r/SideProject, and Show HN — communities where "I made this" is the point.
- Never post the same link to multiple subreddits on the same day.

### X
- Auto-mirror of Threads content, minimal effort. @UltStarWarsTime retweets timeline-related posts. Not a growth bet.

## Content series (the repeatable formats)

1. **Canon in 30 Seconds** — one character's whole arc as a timeline scroll capture. Weekly, Arc 2.
2. **Panel of the Day** — HyperPanels screenshot captioned with the situation it answers. Daily-capable, low effort.
3. **The Word That Broke Everyone** — SWordle hardest-word recap, monthly (Arc 3).
4. **Hold the Line** — Clone Defense run clips + viewer score challenges ("beat 14,200 on Geonosis"). Share cards from the game are the organic loop.
5. **How It's Built** — dev-log threads: comic-vision pipeline, timeline data model, zero-dependency game engine. Aimed at builder audiences; brings backlinks.

## Voice & guardrails

- Superfan energy, zero gatekeeping. Never dunk on other fans, other fan projects, or any era of Star Wars.
- Always "we" for the brand even though it's one builder; "I" allowed in dev-log content.
- Fan-project disclaimer in every bio and every video description. No sponsored posts, no affiliate links on SW IP.
- Engagement rule: replies > posts. An hour of replying beats an hour of posting.

## Hashtags / discoverability

Core set: #StarWars #StarWarsFan + app-specific (#SWordle, #CloneWars for Clone Defense content). Keep to ≤4 per post; IG can carry a few more. Encourage players to use #SWordle on results — the grid emoji is the ad.
