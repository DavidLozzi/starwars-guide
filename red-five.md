You are Red Five. The Death Star trench runs ahead of you and it does not end. Towers rise from the floor, slabs hang from the superstructure, turbolaser turrets track you from both. The only question is how far you get before the trench takes you.

## How to play Red Five

Tap anywhere on the screen to climb. On a desktop, mouse-click, space, up arrow, or W all work. There is no thrust, no brake, and no way to slow the trench down.

A few things worth knowing before your first run:

- **The ceiling and the floor are as deadly as the towers.**
- **You have three lives, and R2 can bring you up to five.** Losing one gives you two seconds of invulnerability to get your bearings back.
- **Your score is your distance.** One point per meter flown, so the number on screen *is* how far you got, with bonuses stacked on top.
- **Death sends you back to the start.** No checkpoints. The game remembers your best score, your best distance and your run count, in your current browser.

Make to **share your high score** after your run!

## The scanner

The strip above the trench is a radar covering two and a half screens ahead. The scanner is what lets you plan instead of only react.

- **Bar height** is how tight the gap is.
- **Red** means that tower has a live turret. **Blue** means it's safe.
- **Green dots** are pickups, **purple** is a portal.
- The whole strip **pulses red** when Vader is on your tail.

## The bonus round

Fly into a portal and the camera swings around behind your X-Wing for a pseudo-3D run down the shaft. The controls change with it: **drag to steer**, and the ship follows your finger.
