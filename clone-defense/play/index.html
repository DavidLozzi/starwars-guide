FIELD MANUAL

OBJECTIVE
The Separatist droid line rains blaster fire on your clone troopers. Hold the line. If every clone falls, you lost the war.
COMMAND THE JEDI
Drag anywhere to guide the Jedi across the field. The saber deflects any bolt that comes near, sending it back into the droids to cut them down.
THE FORCE
The Force gauge charges as you deflect bolts, fell droids, and clear waves. As it climbs, abilities come online in order — PUSH, then BLOCK, then CRUSH, then HEAL (see ABILITIES below). Tap an unlocked ability, drag over the target, and confirm with GO (CANCEL to stand down). Tap the "?" on any ability for its own field note.
ABILITIES
PUSH — shove a droid cluster back and stall their fire.
BLOCK — raise a Force wall to protect your clones; they shoot through it.
CRUSH — kill one droid outright, whatever its armor.
HEAL — restore a wounded clone and top up its neighbors.
BETWEEN WAVES & WORLDS
Clear a wave and the front falls quiet for a breather — your abilities stay live, and the clones still standing hold their ground into the next wave. Clear an entire world and you advance to the next front, where a fresh clone garrison musters at full strength to relieve the line.
